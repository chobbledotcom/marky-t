Thinking of booking me as your DJ? Here are answers to some frequently asked questions to help you plan your event.

Will you just play music, or will you help get our guests involved?

As a personality DJ, I bring more than music to your evening. With experience presenting radio shows and entertaining thousands of people at events across Essex, I’m confident on the microphone and love getting guests involved. I combine those skills with my musical knowledge and a feel for what to play—and when—to create the right atmosphere for your celebration.

Have you DJ’d at many weddings?

Yes! I’ve been a full-time DJ since 2002 and have extensive experience entertaining at weddings and other events.

Can we give you a playlist so you can get a feel for our music tastes?

Absolutely—I love it when couples get involved in the planning!
I’ll work with you to understand your musical tastes and the atmosphere you want throughout the evening. You can also add songs directly to your playlist through your personal online Planning Portal, so it’s easy to include your favourites whenever inspiration strikes.

Do you bring your own equipment, or do we need to supply anything?

I bring everything I need for my DJ setup, including a high-quality sound system and lighting effects. I also carry spare equipment for backup, so you can relax and enjoy the celebration.
